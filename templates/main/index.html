02 / About me

A builder's mindset.
A learner's curiosity.

* Ronald E. McNair Scholar
* University of Louisiana at Lafayette graduate.
* Current master's student at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette.
* Louisiana Army National Guard member.

Alongside my academic and technical experience, I serve in the Army National Guard, where I've developed a strong sense of discipline, teamwork, adaptability, and responsibility.

I enjoy building practical solutions, exploring how systems work behind the scenes, and taking on projects that challenge me to learn something new. My technical experience includes backend development, REST APIs, databases, web applications, and cybersecurity-focused projects.

As a McNair Scholar, I began research focused on blackmail scams and ransomware, exploring how cybercriminals operate, the techniques they use, and patterns that can help improve user awareness and prevention. I have worked at the APRIORI Center since this project began, under the mentorship of Dr. Bhupendra Acharya, who leads the lab. I am continuing this research with him and our team during my graduate studies, expanding on my previous work and exploring new data, connections, and research directions. This experience has strengthened my interest in cybersecurity while allowing me to combine software development, data analysis, and research to investigate real-world security problems.

What I enjoy most about computer science is the problem-solving process. Whether I'm debugging an application, designing a backend system, analyzing data, conducting research, or learning a new technology, I like taking a complex problem and working through it piece by piece until I find a solution.

I'm continuing to expand my skills through graduate study, research, and personal projects, and this portfolio is a collection of some of the work, research, and ideas I've developed along the way.
